# ✈️ Airline Satisfaction Analysis 

## 📌 Problem Statement
Customer satisfaction is critical in the highly competitive airline industry. This project conducts exploratory data analysis (EDA) on a dataset of over 120,000 airline passengers to uncover insights into the factors influencing satisfaction and dissatisfaction. The ultimate goal is to generate actionable recommendations for enhancing customer experience.

## 📊 Dataset Summary
The dataset includes a variety of demographic and service-related variables, such as:

- **Categorical Variables (5):** Gender, Customer Type, Type of Travel, Class, Satisfaction  
- **Numerical Variables (19):** Age, Flight Distance, Delays, Ratings (Online Booking, Check-in, Boarding, Service, etc.)

## 🔍 Analysis Objectives
This project addresses the following key questions:

1. How many categorical and numerical variables are present?
2. What are the descriptive statistics (mean, mode, std, min, max) of numerical variables?
3. What percentage of passengers are satisfied? How does this vary by customer type and travel purpose?
4. What is the profile of a repeat airline passenger?
5. Does flight distance influence customer satisfaction and travel behavior?
6. Which factors most impact customer satisfaction and dissatisfaction?

## 📈 Key Findings
- **Satisfaction Rate:** 43.45% of passengers are satisfied overall.
- **Business Travelers:** 58.37% of business travel customers are satisfied.
- **Repeat Passengers:** More likely to be older, female, travel for business, and fly business class.
- **Flight Distance:** Longer flights (>2,000 miles) correlate with higher satisfaction.
- **Top Satisfaction Drivers:** Flight distance, older age, and schedule convenience.
- **Top Dissatisfaction Drivers:** Online booking difficulty, in-flight services, and younger travelers.
